t. is an unofficial Telegram client for iPhone and iPad, based on the open-source Telegram, Nagram and Nextgram projects.

Keep your Telegram conversations together while making the interface work the way you prefer.

• Personalize your chats with themes and appearance controls.
• Organize conversations with folders and chat filters.
• Use translation and voice-message transcription tools.
• Manage multiple Telegram accounts.
• Explore extra controls for messages, media, privacy and navigation.

A Telegram account is required. Optional translation and transcription services may require separate provider credentials. Feature availability depends on Telegram and your configured providers.

t. is independent of Telegram Messenger Inc., Nagram and Nextgram, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by their developers. Telegram is a trademark of Telegram Messenger Inc.
